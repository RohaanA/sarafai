SarafAI is an alternate credit-scoring and cash-flow intelligence platform for Pakistan's unbanked micro-merchants. Over 70% of Pakistan's economy is informal: kiryana store owners have no bank statements, salary slips or collateral, so banks can't underwrite them and millions of viable businesses are locked out of formal credit.

SarafAI turns the data these merchants already produce — photos of paper receipts and supplier invoices, handwritten khata ledger notes, and mobile wallet histories (Easypaisa/JazzCash) — into a bankable financial identity. Our pipeline ingests these documents, extracts transactions with OCR (Qwen-VL, Urdu + English), reconciles them into a single verified ledger on Alibaba Cloud PostgreSQL/AnalyticDB, and auto-generates an SME Credit Health Score (300–900). The score is deliberately explainable, built from six transparent cash-flow factors — revenue scale, consistency, growth trend, business activity, digital documentation and recency — so merchants can see exactly how to improve it, and lenders can audit every point. Fintechs and banks consume the score via an API to originate collateral-free micro-loans sized to real, verified cash flows.

We built a fully working MVP: a zero-dependency web app demonstrating the complete loop — scan a receipt, watch OCR extract the fields, confirm, and see the credit score and loan offer recalculate live. Impact: unlocks micro-loans for unbanked MSMEs, lowers default rates for lenders through data-driven underwriting, and organically documents informal commerce into the tax net — one scanned receipt at a time.
